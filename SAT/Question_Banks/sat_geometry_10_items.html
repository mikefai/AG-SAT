Q1 — Triangle (Easy)

Sides 6, 8, 10. Angle opposite 10?

  • A. 30°
  • B. 90°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 45°
Answer: B. 6-8-10 Pythagorean). C 60 wrong; D 45 wrong; A opposite small.

Q2 — Area (Medium)

Rectangle 12 by 8; circle diameter 4 inside; find area outside circle.

  • A. 92
  • B. 96 - 4π
  • C. 96 - 16π
  • D. 96 - 2π
Answer: B. area rect=96; circle r=2 => 4π). C r=4 error; D r=√2 error.

Q3 — Coordinate Distance (Medium)

Points (1,2) and (4,6). Distance?

  • A. 6
  • B. 5
  • C. 7
  • D. √34
Answer: B. √((4−1)²+(6−2)²) = √(9+16) = 5. A mis-adds; C sums coordinates; D mis-squares.)

Q4 — Circle (Medium)

Circumference = 10π. Radius?

  • A. 25
  • B. 5
  • C. 10
  • D. 20
Answer: B. D=2πr => r=5). C = D/π; D = 2C/π; A wrong.

Q5 — Similar Triangles (Medium)

Triangle sides 3,4,5; scaled by 2. New sides?

  • A. 5,6,7
  • B. 6,8,10
  • C. 3,4,10
  • D. 6,4,5
Answer: B. C only last; D mixed; A wrong scale.

Q6 — Trigonometry (Medium)

Right triangle: angle 30°, hypotenuse 10. Opposite side?

  • A. 10
  • B. 5
  • C. 5√3
  • D. 10√3
Answer: B. sin30=0.5 => opp=5). C adjacent; D wrong; A inverse.

Q7 — Volume (Medium)

Cube side 3. Volume?

  • A. 36
  • B. 27
  • C. 9
  • D. 18
Answer: B. 3³). C area; D 2area; A 4area.

Q8 — Angles / Parallel Lines (Medium)

Lines parallel; transversal angle 65°. Corresponding angle?

  • A. 90°
  • B. 65°
  • C. 115°
  • D. 25°
Answer: B. corresponding = equal). C supplementary; D complement; A wrong.

Q9 — Coordinate Slope / Line (Hard)

Line through (1,3) slope 2. y-intercept?

  • A. 5
  • B. 1
  • C. 2
  • D. 3
Answer: B. 3 = 2*1 + b => b=1). C slope; D point; A 3+2.

Q10 — Circle Area + Triangle (Hard)

Equilateral triangle inscribed in circle radius 2. Find area.

  • A. 3√2
  • B. 6
  • C. 3√3
  • D. 6√3
Answer: C. side s = R√3 = 2√3; area = (√3/4)s² = (√3/4)(12) = 3√3. D doubles; A uses √2; B drops the root.)

Q11 — Right Triangle Legs (Easy)

A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5. Other leg?

  • A. 12
  • B. 8
  • C. 10
  • D. 18
Answer: A. √(169−25) = √144 = 12). B subtracts; C guesses; D adds.

Q12 — Square Area (Easy)

Square with side 9. Area?

  • A. 18
  • B. 72
  • C. 81
  • D. 36
Answer: C. 9²). D gives perimeter; A halves; B mis-multiplies.

Q13 — Complementary Angles (Medium)

Two complementary angles; one is 35°. The other?

  • A. 125°
  • B. 35°
  • C. 55°
  • D. 145°
Answer: C. 90−35). D uses supplementary; A adds; B repeats.

Q14 — Circle Area From Diameter (Medium)

Diameter 10. Area?

  • A. 10π
  • B. 50π
  • C. 25π
  • D. 100π
Answer: C. r=5 → 25π). D uses diameter as radius; A gives circumference; B halves wrongly.

Q15 — 30-60-90 Sides (Hard)

Short leg 7 in a 30-60-90 triangle. Hypotenuse?

  • A. 21
  • B. 49
  • C. 14
  • D. 7√3
Answer: C. hypotenuse = 2× short leg). D gives the long leg; A triples; B squares.

Q16 — Rectangle Perimeter (Easy)

Length 15, width 7. Perimeter?

  • A. 22
  • B. 30
  • C. 44
  • D. 105
Answer: C. 2×(15+7)). D gives area; A adds once; B doubles length only.

Q17 — Parallel Slopes (Medium)

Line M has slope −2/3. A parallel line has slope?

  • A. −2
  • B. 3
  • C. 2
  • D. −3
Answer: A. Parallel = equal slopes. B is the perpendicular; C flips sign; D inverts.

Q18 — Triangle Area (Medium)

Base 12, height 9. Area?

  • A. 21
  • B. 27
  • C. 54
  • D. 108
Answer: C. ½×12×9). D forgets the half; A adds; B quarters.

Q19 — Arc Length (Hard)

Circle radius 6; central angle 60°. Arc length?

  • A. 6π
  • B. π
  • C. 2π
  • D. 12π
Answer: C. 60/360 × 2π×6 = 2π). D gives full circumference; A halves it; B divides by 12.

Q20 — Midpoint on Grid (Easy)

Endpoints (0, 0) and (8, 12). Midpoint?

  • A. (4, 12)
  • B. (8, 6)
  • C. (4, 6)
  • D. (8, 12)
Answer: C. Halve each coordinate. D repeats an endpoint; A/B halve only one.

Q21 — Volume of Box (Medium)

Box: 4 × 5 × 10. Volume?

  • A. 100
  • B. 38
  • C. 95
  • D. 200
Answer: D. 4×5×10). A halves; B sums edges; C mis-multiplies.

Q22 — Supplementary Angle (Easy)

An angle measures 110°. Its supplement?

  • A. 20°
  • B. 250°
  • C. 110°
  • D. 70°
Answer: D. 180−110). A uses 90; B adds; C repeats.

Q23 — cos Ratio (Medium)

Right triangle: adjacent 5, hypotenuse 13. cos(θ)?

  • A. 12
  • B. 5
  • C. 13
  • D. 5
Answer: D. adjacent/hypotenuse). A gives sin; B gives tan; C inverts.

Q24 — Circle Through Three Points... (Hard)

A circle centered at (0,0) passes through (3, 4). Radius?

  • A. 5
  • B. 7
  • C. 25
  • D. 12
Answer: A. √(9+16) = 5). B adds; C skips the root; D sums wrongly.

Q25 — Isosceles Angles (Medium)

Isosceles triangle: vertex angle 40°. Each base angle?

  • A. 140°
  • B. 100°
  • C. 40°
  • D. 70°
Answer: D. (180−40)/2). A subtracts from 180 wrong; B uses 200; C repeats vertex.

Q26 — Slope-Intercept Check (Easy)

Which point lies on y = 3x − 7?

  • A. (0, 7)
  • B. (3, 7)
  • C. (2, 3)
  • D. (3, 2)
Answer: D. 3×3−7 = 2). A flips intercept; B mis-evaluates; C swaps coordinates.

Q27 — Cylinder Volume (Hard)

Cylinder: radius 3, height 8. Volume?

  • A. 48π
  • B. 24π
  • C. 144π
  • D. 72π
Answer: D. π×9×8). A uses diameter 3; B halves; C doubles.

Q28 — 45-45-90 Hypotenuse (Medium)

Legs of length 6. Hypotenuse?

  • A. 12
  • B. 6√3
  • C. 36
  • D. 6√2
Answer: D. leg×√2). A doubles; B uses 30-60-90 ratio; C squares.

Q29 — Distance Check (Medium)

Points (0, 3) and (4, 0). Distance?

  • A. 7
  • B. 12
  • C. 25
  • D. 5
Answer: D. √(16+9) = 5). A adds; B multiplies; C skips the root.

Q30 — Area of Triangle on Grid (Easy)

Right triangle with legs 5 and 8. Area?

  • A. 20
  • B. 40
  • C. 13
  • D. 10
Answer: A. ½×5×8). B forgets half; C adds; D quarters.

Q31 — Vertical Angles (Easy)

Two intersecting lines form a 65° angle. Its vertical opposite?

  • A. 65°
  • B. 115°
  • C. 25°
  • D. 295°
Answer: A. Vertical angles are equal. B gives the adjacent supplement; C complements; D is reflex nonsense.

Q32 — Perimeter of Triangle (Medium)

Sides 7, 10, 15. Perimeter?

  • A. 32
  • B. 30
  • C. 105
  • D. 16
Answer: A. 7+10+15). B rounds; C multiplies (area-style); D halves.

Q33 — tan Ratio (Medium)

Opposite 8, adjacent 15. tan(θ)?

  • A. 8
  • B. 15
  • C. 8
  • D. 15
Answer: A. opposite/adjacent). B inverts; C/D bring in the hypotenuse 17.

Q34 — Sector Area (Hard)

Circle radius 4; sector angle 90°. Sector area?

  • A. 4π
  • B. 16π
  • C. 8π
  • D. 2π
Answer: A. 90/360 × 16π = 4π). B gives full circle; C halves circle; D quarters again.

Q35 — Reflection (Medium)

Point (5, −2) reflected across the x-axis. New point?

  • A. (5, 2)
  • B. (−5, −2)
  • C. (−5, 2)
  • D. (2, 5)
Answer: A. x-axis flips y's sign. B reflects across y-axis; C reflects through origin; D swaps coordinates.
