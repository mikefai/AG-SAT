Q1 — Linear Solve (Easy)

3x + 7 = 22

  • A. 8
  • B. x = 5
  • C. 6
  • D. 7
Answer: B. 3x=15, x=5. Distractor: 22/3 ≈ 7 (division error).

Q2 — System Substitution (Medium)

y = 2x + 3; y = -x + 9

  • A. 13
  • B. 7
  • C. 9
  • D. 11
Answer: B. solve 2x+3=-x+9 => 3x=6 => x=2 => y=7). Distractor: y=9 (uses second equation at wrong x).

Q3 — Function Evaluation (Medium)

f(x) = 3x - 4; find f(5)

  • A. 8
  • B. 11
  • C. 15
  • D. 19
Answer: B. 35-4=11). C = 35+4? D = 3*5+4 error.

Q4 — Slope (Medium)

Line through (2,4) and (5,10). Slope?

  • A. 1.5
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 2.5
Answer: B. 6/3=2). C = 6; D = 6/3 mistaken; A = 3/2 reverse.

Q5 — Linear Inequality (Medium)

2x - 3 > 7. Solution?

  • A. x > 7
  • B. x > 5
  • C. x > 2
  • D. x < 2
Answer: B. 2x > 10 => x > 5). C = 7/3; D opposite; A no division.

Q6 — Ratio / Proportion (Medium)

3 workers finish in 8 hrs; how long for 6 workers (same rate)?

  • A. 16
  • B. 4 hrs
  • C. 6
  • D. 12
Answer: B. Inverse relation; distractor 12 (direct error).

Q7 — Parallel Lines (Hard)

Line A: y = 2x + 1. Line B parallel through (3,5). Find B eq.

  • A. y=2x-1
  • B. 2x+1
  • C. y=-0.5x+...
  • D. y=2x+5
Answer: A. parallel => same slope 2; 5=2*3+b => b=-1). D uses wrong b calc.

Q8 — Word Problem (Hard)

Car starts at 40 mph, accelerates 10 mph/min; speed after 4 min?

  • A. 70
  • B. 80
  • C. 90
  • D. 100
Answer: B. 40 + 10*4 = 80). A = 40+30; C = 40+50; D wrong rate.

Q9 — Function Intersection (Medium)

f(x)=x²-4; g(x)=x+2. Find the sum of all x-values where f(x)=g(x).

  • A. 5
  • B. 1
  • C. -2
  • D. 3
Answer: B. x²-4=x+2 → x²-x-6=0 → (x-3)(x+2)=0 → x=3 or x=-2 → sum=1). C and D give only one root; A adds magnitudes incorrectly.

Q10 — Linear Modeling (Easy)

Cost per item $3 plus $15 fee; total for 10 items?

  • A. $30
  • B. $45
  • C. $60
  • D. $75
Answer: B. 3×10+15=45). A omits the fee; C doubles the per-item count; D adds an extra fee.

Q11 — Solve With Fractions (Easy)

5x − 9 = 2x + 12

  • A. 3
  • B. 9
  • C. 7
  • D. 5
Answer: C. 3x=21 → x=7). D subtracts wrong; A divides by 7; B adds instead of subtracting.

Q12 — System by Elimination (Medium)

2x + y = 11; x − y = 4. Find x.

  • A. 7
  • B. 4
  • C. 5
  • D. 3
Answer: C. add: 3x=15 → x=5). D gives y; A adds wrong; B picks a constant.

Q13 — Evaluate Function (Medium)

g(t) = −2t + 9; find g(−3).

  • A. −15
  • B. 6
  • C. 15
  • D. 3
Answer: C. −2×−3+9 = 6+9 = 15). D drops a sign; A flips both; B computes 9−3.

Q14 — Slope From Intercept Form (Easy)

Line: y = −4x + 11. Slope?

  • A. 4
  • B. −11
  • C. −4
  • D. 11
Answer: C. D names the intercept; A drops the sign; B negates the intercept.

Q15 — Inequality (Medium)

−3x + 8 < 20. Solution?

  • A. x > 4
  • B. x < 4
  • C. x > −4
  • D. x < −4
Answer: C. −3x < 12 → flip → x > −4). D forgets the flip; A/B mishandle 12/3.

Q16 — Direct Proportion (Medium)

A printer makes 45 pages in 3 minutes. How many in 11 minutes at the same rate?

  • A. 165
  • B. 135
  • C. 150
  • D. 180
Answer: A. 15/min × 11 = 165). B uses 9 min; C uses 10 min; D uses 12 min.

Q17 — Perpendicular Line (Hard)

Line L: y = 3x − 2. A perpendicular line through (0, 5) has equation?

  • A. y = −(1
  • B. y = 3x + 5
  • C. y = −3x + 5
  • D. y = (1
Answer: A. slope −1/3, intercept 5). B keeps parallel slope; C flips sign fully; D uses positive reciprocal.

Q18 — Two-Step Word Problem (Medium)

A taxi charges $4 plus $2.50 per mile. A ride costs $24. How many miles?

  • A. 8
  • B. 9.6
  • C. 6
  • D. 11
Answer: A. 2.5m = 20 → m = 8). B forgets the flag fee; C subtracts wrong; D adds the fee.

Q19 — System: No Solution (Hard)

kx + 2y = 8; 3x + 6y = 12. For no solution, k = ?

  • A. 3
  • B. 0
  • C. 1
  • D. 2
Answer: C. parallel: k/3 = 2/6 → k = 1, with different constant ratio 8/12). D/A break parallelism; B removes x.

Q20 — Function From Table (Easy)

x: 0→4, 2→10. Linear f has f(0)=4, f(2)=10. Find f(1).

  • A. 8
  • B. 5
  • C. 7
  • D. 6
Answer: C. slope 3 → f(1) = 4+3 = 7). D averages endpoints wrong; A uses f(2)−2; B halves f(2).

Q21 — Absolute Value (Medium)

|x − 5| = 9. Find the smaller solution.

  • A. 14
  • B. −14
  • C. −4
  • D. 4
Answer: C. x = 14 or x = −4; smaller is −4). D drops the sign; A takes the larger; B doubles the sign error.

Q22 — Intercepts (Medium)

Line: 4x − 5y = 20. x-intercept?

  • A. −4
  • B. 20
  • C. 5
  • D. 4
Answer: C. set y=0 → 4x=20 → x=5). D uses the coefficient; A signs wrong; B skips division.

Q23 — Equivalent Forms (Hard)

Which is equivalent to 3(x − 4) + 2(2x + 1)?

  • A. 5x − 10
  • B. 7x − 14
  • C. 5x − 2
  • D. 7x − 10
Answer: D. 3x−12+4x+2 = 7x−10). A drops an x-term; B mis-adds constants; C under-distributes.

Q24 — Rate Word Problem (Medium)

Water flows at 6 liters per minute. How long to fill a 150-liter tank?

  • A. 30 min
  • B. 20 min
  • C. 15 min
  • D. 25 min
Answer: D. 150/6 = 25). A divides by 5; B divides by 7.5; C halves.

Q25 — Compare Slopes (Easy)

Lines: y = 2x + 1 and y = 5x − 3. Which rises faster?

  • A. The first line
  • B. Neither rises
  • C. Both rise equally
  • D. The second line
Answer: D. slope 5 > 2). A reverses the comparison; B denies positive slopes; C equates 2 and 5.

Q26 — Literal Equation (Medium)

Solve P = 2l + 2w for l.

  • A. l = P − 2w
  • B. l = P
  • C. l = 2P − w
  • D. l = (P − 2w)
Answer: D. Subtract 2w, divide by 2. A skips division; B flips the sign; C doubles wrongly.

Q27 — System Word Problem (Hard)

Adult tickets $9, child tickets $5. 200 tickets sold for $1,480. How many adult tickets?

  • A. 80
  • B. 100
  • C. 140
  • D. 120
Answer: D. 9a+5(200−a)=1480 → 4a=480 → a=120). A gives child count; B splits evenly; C overshoots.

Q28 — Midpoint (Medium)

Segment endpoints (2, 8) and (10, 4). Midpoint?

  • A. (6, 2)
  • B. (12, 12)
  • C. (4, 6)
  • D. (6, 6)
Answer: D. (2+10)/2, (8+4)/2). A halves y wrong; B sums; C quarters.

Q29 — Inequality Word Problem (Easy)

A club needs at least $300. It has $120 and earns $15 per car wash. Minimum washes?

  • A. 12
  • B. 10
  • C. 20
  • D. 8
Answer: A. 15n ≥ 180 → n ≥ 12). B rounds down; C uses full 300; D halves.

Q30 — Identify Slope Sign (Easy)

A line falls left to right and crosses (0, 6). Which equation fits?

  • A. y = −2x + 6
  • B. y = 2x + 6
  • C. y = −2x − 6
  • D. y = 6x + 2
Answer: A. Falling = negative slope; intercept 6. B rises; C has wrong intercept; D swaps roles.

Q31 — Compound Inequality (Hard)

1 < 2x − 5 ≤ 11. Integer solutions count?

  • A. 6
  • B. 4
  • C. 3
  • D. 5
Answer: D. 3 < x ≤ 8 → x = 4,5,6,7,8 → five values). A includes 3; B drops an endpoint; C counts half.

Q32 — Unit Conversion (Medium)

A car travels 72 km in 48 minutes. Speed in km/h?

  • A. 90
  • B. 75
  • C. 100
  • D. 60
Answer: A. 72 ÷ 0.8 = 90). B divides by wrong time; C rounds up; D uses 72 min.

Q33 — No Solution Check (Medium)

System: y = 4x + 1; y = 4x + 9. How many solutions?

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. Infinitely many
  • D. 0
Answer: D. Same slope, different intercepts = parallel. A/B assume crossing; C needs identical lines.

Q34 — Percent Markup (Easy)

A $60 item is marked up 25%. New price?

  • A. $75
  • B. $85
  • C. $65
  • D. $70
Answer: A. 60×1.25 = 75). B adds 25 flat; C adds 5; D adds 10.

Q35 — Choose Model (Hard)

A population doubles every 3 years, starting at 500. Which gives P after t years?

  • A. P = 500·2^(t
  • B. P = 500·2^(3t)
  • C. P = 500 + 2t
  • D. P = 500·3^t
Answer: A. Doubling per 3 years = exponent t/3. B inverts the period; C is linear; D uses base 3.
