## Q1 — Quadratic Solve (Easy)

x² - 5x + 6 = 0

- A. 5,6

- B. 2,3

- C. -2,-3

- D. 1,6

**Answer:** B. factors to (x-2)(x-3)). C wrong signs; A sum=11.

---

## Q2 — Quadratic Formula (Medium)

2x² + 4x - 6 = 0. Sum of roots?

- A. -3

- B. -2

- C. 2

- D. 3

**Answer:** B. sum = -b/a = -4/2 = -2). C opposite sign; D product.

---

## Q3 — Nonlinear System (Medium)

y = x²; y = 2x + 3. Find solutions count.

- A. 0

- B. 1

- C. 2

- D. None

**Answer:** C. intersections at x=-1,3 => two). A misses; B one error.

## Q4 — Function Inverse (Hard)

f(x)=2x+4. Find f⁻¹(10).

- A. 2

- B. 3

- C. 4

- D. 5

**Answer:** B. y=2x+4 => x=(y-4)/2 => (10-4)/2=3). A = 10/2 no -4; C = 10-4; D wrong.

- A. (-3,-7)

**Answer:** B. C wrong signs; D wrong y sign; A both wrong.

## Q6 — Complex / Rational (Hard)

(3x+2)/(x-1) = 4. Find x.

- A. 2

- B. 1.5

- C. 3

- D. 6

**Answer:** B. 3x+2=4x-4 => x=6? Wait: 3x+2 = 4(x-1) => 3x+2=4x-4 => x=6 => D. Let me correct: x=6 => answer D. Actually 3x+2=4x-4 => -x=-6 => x=6. So D.)

## Q7 — Exponents (Medium)

(2³)² / 2⁴ = ?

- A. 16

- B. 2

- C. 4

- D. 8

**Answer:** B. 2⁶/2⁴=2²=4? Wait 6-4=2 => 2²=4 => C. Actually (8)²=64; 64/16=4 => C.)

## Q8 — Polynomial Root (Medium)

If x=2 is a root of x³−2x²−x+2=0, which of the following is another real root?

- A. −3

- B. 1

- C. 0

- D. 3

**Answer:** B. factoring out (x−2) leaves x²−1=0, so x=±1; only 1 is listed. C/D/A are not roots.)

f(x)=√(x-3). Domain?

- A. all real

- B. x ≥ 3

- C. x > 3

- D. x ≤ 3

**Answer:** B. C excludes 3; D opposite; A wrong.

## Q10 — Nonlinear Model (Easy)

Area of square = x²; if side increases by 2, area increases by 44. Find original side.

- A. 8

- B. 10

- C. 9

- D. 11

**Answer:** B. (x+2)² - x² = 4x+4 = 44 => x=10). C = 9 => 44? No; D too high.

## Q11 — Factor Simple Quadratic (Easy)

x² + 7x + 12 = 0. Roots?

- A. −6 and −2

- B. 6 and 2

- C. −3 and −4

- D. 3 and 4

**Answer:** C. (x+3)(x+4)). D flips signs; A/B give wrong products.

## Q12 — Sum of Roots (Medium)

3x² − 12x + 7 = 0. Sum of roots?

- A. 7

- B. 12

- C. 4

- D. −4

**Answer:** C. −b/a = 12/3 = 4). D flips sign; A gives product; B ignores division.

## Q13 — Vertex (Medium)

y = (x + 5)² − 9. Vertex?

- A. (−5, 9)

- B. (5, 9)

- C. (−5, −9)

- D. (5, −9)

**Answer:** C. Form (x−h)²+k with h=−5, k=−9. D flips h; A flips k; B flips both.

## Q14 — Intersections With Axis (Easy)

f(x) = x² − 9. x-intercepts?

- A. 0 and 9

- B. 3 only

- C. 3 and −3

- D. 9 and −9

**Answer:** C. x²=9). D takes roots of 81; A mixes in y-intercept; B drops a root.

## Q15 — Complete the Square (Hard)

x² + 6x + 5 = 0. Which is an equivalent vertex form?

- A. (x − 3)² = 4

- B. (x + 6)² = 31

- C. (x + 3)² = 4

- D. (x + 3)² = −4

**Answer:** C. (x+3)² −9+5=0 → (x+3)²=4). D signs wrong; A centers wrong; B mis-completes.

---

## Q16 — Rational Equation (Medium)

5/x = 15/9. Find x.

- A. 9

- B. 15

- C. 3

- D. 5

**Answer:** C. 5/x = 5/3 → x=3). D copies numerator; A copies denominator; B copies 15.

## Q17 — Exponent Rules (Medium)

(3⁴)(3²) / 3³ = ?

- A. 81

- B. 3

- C. 27

- D. 9

**Answer:** C. 3^(4+2−3) = 3³ = 27). D subtracts once more; A adds; B divides twice.

## Q18 — Function Composition (Hard)

f(x) = 2x + 1, g(x) = x². Find g(f(2)).

- A. 9

- B. 5

- C. 25

- D. 11

**Answer:** C. f(2)=5 → g(5)=25). D computes f(g(2)); A squares then doubles wrong; B stops at f(2).

## Q19 — Zeros of Factored Form (Easy)

h(x) = (x − 7)(x + 2). Zeros?

- A. −7 and 2

- B. 7 and 2

**Answer:** D. Set each factor to zero. A flips both; B/C flip one each.

## Q20 — Domain of Fraction (Medium)

f(x) = 4/(x + 6). Excluded value?

- A. x = 6

- B. x = 4

**Answer:** D. Denominator zero at −6. A flips sign; B uses numerator; C assumes zero.

## Q21 — Discriminant (Hard)

x² + 4x + 4 = 0. How many real solutions?

- A. 2

- B. 0

- D. 1 (repeated)

**Answer:** D. discriminant 16−16=0 → one repeated root −2). A assumes two; B misreads; C impossible for quadratics.

## Q31 — Shifted Parabola (Hard)

y = x² shifted 4 left and 1 down. Equation?

- A. y = (x + 4)² − 1

- B. y = (x − 4)² − 1

- C. y = (x + 4)² + 1

- D. y = x² − 4x − 1

**Answer:** A. Left = +4 inside, down = −1 outside. B shifts right; C shifts up; D expands wrongly.

## Q35 — Model With Quadratic (Hard)

A ball's height: h = −5t² + 20t. When does it hit the ground (t > 0)?

- A. t = 4

- B. t = 5

- C. t = 20

- D. t = 2

**Answer:** A. −5t(t−4)=0 → t=0 or 4). B uses 20/4 wrong; C takes coefficient; D halves.
