Problem Solving & Data Analysis — 35 Practice Items

Q1 — Mean / SD (Easy)

Data: 10, 12, 14, 16. Mean?

  • A. 15
  • B. 13
  • C. 14
  • D. 12
Answer: B. 52/4=13). C = 14 (median); D = low; A wrong sum.

Q2 — Probability (Medium)

A standard deck has 52 cards, 13 of which are hearts. If one card is drawn at random, what is the probability it is a heart?

  • A. 1
  • B. 13
  • C. 1
  • D. 1
Answer: D. 13/52 = 1/4. A uses the 13 suits as denominator; B removes hearts from the deck; C assumes two suits.)

Q3 — Conditional Probability (Medium)

P(rain)=40%, P(cloudy|rain)=80%. Find P(rain and cloudy).

  • A. 0.08
  • B. 0.32
  • C. 0.40
  • D. 0.12
Answer: B. (0.4)x(0.8)=0.32. C ignores the condition; D subtracts; A multiplies wrongly.)

Q4 — Data Table (Medium)

Year 2019-2023 sales: 100, 120, 140, 160, 180. What was the percent increase from 2019 to 2020?

  • A. 25%
  • B. 20%
  • C. 10%
  • D. 15%
Answer: B. (120-100)/100 = 20%. C halves; D and A misread other years' gains.)

Q5 — Statistics / Z (Hard)

Mean=50, SD=10. Value 70. Z-score?

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 0.5
Answer: B. 20/10=2). A half; C triple; D wrong.

Q6 — Ratio / Percent (Medium)

Recipe: 3 cups flour per 2 cups sugar; need 9 cups flour, how much sugar?

  • A. 7.5
  • B. 6
  • C. 4.5
  • D. 3
Answer: B. 3:2 => 9:6). C = 9/2; D = 3; A = 9*5/6 error.

Q7 — Data Interpretation (Medium)

Scatter positive trend; outlier above trend line. What does outlier show?

  • A. Error
  • B. Higher value than trend predicts
  • C. No correlation
  • D. Negative
Answer: B. A possible but not best; C contradicts trend; D wrong direction.

Q8 — Rate / Work (Medium)

Machine A does job in 6 hrs; B in 3 hrs. Together?

  • A. 2.5
  • B. 3
  • C. 9
  • D. 2 hrs
Answer: D. 1/6+1/3=1/2 => 2 hrs). A sum/2 wrong; B max; C sum.

Q9 — Proportion (Hard)

Sample of 500, 35% prefer brand X. Margin of error ±3%. Best estimate true %?

  • A. 35±1
  • B. 32-38%
  • C. 35% exact
  • D. 30-40%
Answer: B. 35±3 = 32-38). C ignores MOE; D too wide; A wrong margin.

Q10 — Data / Percentage Change (Easy)

Price $400 to $480. % increase?

  • A. 10%
  • B. 20%
  • C. 16.7%
  • D. 25%
Answer: B. 80/400=20%). C = 80/480; D = 100/400; A = 40/400.

Q11 — Median (Easy)

Data: 4, 9, 7, 21, 15. Median?

  • A. 11
  • B. 9
  • C. 7
  • D. 15
Answer: B. ordered 4,7,9,15,21 → middle 9). C takes second; D takes fourth; A averages ends.

Q12 — Simple Probability (Easy)

A bag has 6 red and 4 blue marbles. P(blue)?

  • A. 2
  • B. 4
  • C. 1
  • D. 6
Answer: A. 4/10 = 2/5). B inverts; C assumes equal; D gives red's probability.

Q13 — Percent of Total (Medium)

A class of 200: 45% walk to school. How many walk?

  • A. 90
  • B. 100
  • C. 110
  • D. 45
Answer: A. 0.45×200 = 90). B halves the class; C uses 55%; D copies the percent.

Q14 — Average Speed (Medium)

120 miles in 2.5 hours. Speed?

  • A. 60 mph
  • B. 45 mph
  • C. 48 mph
  • D. 50 mph
Answer: C. 120/2.5 = 48). D divides by 2.4; A halves time; B subtracts.

Q15 — Margin of Error (Medium)

Poll: 48% support ± 4%. Which range is consistent?

  • A. 40–56%
  • B. 46–50%
  • C. 44–52%
  • D. 48% exactly
Answer: C. 48±4). D ignores margin; A doubles it; B halves it.

Q16 — Two-Way Table (Hard)

200 surveyed: 90 own dogs; of dog owners, 30 also own cats; 60 own cats total. How many own neither?

  • A. 110
  • B. 30
  • C. 80
  • D. 60
Answer: C. dogs-or-cats = 90+60−30 = 120; neither = 200−120 = 80). D gives cats total; A adds wrong; B gives overlap.

Q17 — Percent Decrease (Easy)

$50 shirt on sale for $40. Percent off?

  • A. 10%
  • B. 80%
  • C. 20%
  • D. 25%
Answer: C. 10/50 = 20%). D uses 10/40; A halves; B gives the paid share.

Q18 — Expected Value (Medium)

A game pays $10 with probability 0.3, else $0. Expected payout?

  • A. $3
  • B. $10
  • C. $7
  • D. $0.30
Answer: A. 10×0.3). B ignores probability; C subtracts; D misplaces decimal.

Q19 — Line of Best Fit (Medium)

Scatterplot rises left to right; one point sits far above the trend.

Question: What does that point represent?

  • A. A negative trend.
  • B. An error that must be deleted.
  • C. A value higher than the trend predicts.
  • D. Proof of no correlation.
Answer: C. Outliers are described relative to trend. D contradicts the rise; A flips direction; B prescribes deletion.

Q20 — Ratio Split (Easy)

Split $90 in ratio 2:3. Larger share?

  • A. $45
  • B. $36
  • C. $54
  • D. $60
Answer: C. 5 parts → $18 each → 3×18 = 54). D uses 2:1; A halves; B gives the smaller share.

Q21 — Growth Factor (Medium)

A population grows 5% yearly from 2,000. After 1 year?

  • A. 2,100
  • B. 2,050
  • C. 2,500
  • D. 2,005
Answer: A. 2000×1.05). B uses 2.5%; C uses 25%; D adds 5 flat.

Q22 — Conditional Probability (Hard)

70% of days are sunny. On sunny days, 40% have high pollen. P(sunny AND high pollen)?

  • A. 0.70
  • B. 1.10
  • C. 0.28
  • D. 0.40
Answer: C. 0.7×0.4). D drops the first stage; A drops the second; B adds probabilities.

Q23 — Mean With Outlier (Medium)

Scores: 70, 72, 74, 76, 100. Which statement is true?

  • A. Median exceeds mean.
  • B. Range is 4.
  • C. The mean exceeds the median.
  • D. Mean equals median.
Answer: C. mean 78.4 > median 74; outlier pulls mean up). D/A ignore skew; B miscomputes (100−70=30).

Q24 — Unit Rate (Easy)

12 gallons to drive 300 miles. Miles per gallon?

  • A. 30
  • B. 36
  • C. 25
  • D. 20
Answer: C. 300/12 = 25). D divides by 15; A divides by 10; B adds.

Q25 — Percent Increase (Medium)

Membership grew from 400 to 460. Percent increase?

  • A. 13%
  • B. 60%
  • C. 6%
  • D. 15%
Answer: D. 60/400 = 15%). A uses 60/460; B takes the raw gain; C divides by 1000.

Q26 — Sampling (Hard)

A survey of shoppers at one luxury mall claims citywide income rose. Main flaw?

  • A. Unrepresentative sample for a citywide claim.
  • B. Too many shoppers surveyed.
  • C. Income cannot be measured.
  • D. Malls have no shoppers.
Answer: A. One luxury mall ≠ the city. B inverts the issue; C denies measurement; D is absurd.

Q27 — Combined Work (Medium)

A fills a pool in 4 hours, B in 6 hours. Together?

  • A. 2.4 hours
  • B. 5 hours
  • C. 10 hours
  • D. 3 hours
Answer: A. 1/4+1/6 = 5/12 → 12/5 = 2.4). B averages times; C adds; D guesses half of 6.

Q28 — Reading a Chart (Easy)

Bar chart: apples 30, bananas 45, cherries 25. Most popular?

  • A. Apples
  • B. Cherries
  • C. All tied
  • D. Bananas
Answer: D. Tallest bar 45. A/B pick shorter bars; C ignores differences.

Q29 — Proportional Tax (Medium)

Tax is 8% on $250. Total with tax?

  • A. $258
  • B. $280
  • C. $250
  • D. $270
Answer: D. 250×1.08 = 270). A adds 8 flat; B uses 12%; C forgets tax.

Q30 — Standard Deviation (Hard)

Two classes take the same test with equal means. Class A scores cluster near the mean; class B spreads widely. Which has the larger SD?

  • A. Class B
  • B. Class A
  • C. They are equal
  • D. Cannot compare
Answer: A. Spread = larger SD by definition. B picks the cluster; C ignores spread; D denies a direct comparison.

Q31 — Discount Chain (Hard)

$200 jacket: 20% off, then 10% off the sale price. Final price?

  • A. $140
  • B. $120
  • C. $160
  • D. $144
Answer: D. 200×0.8=160; 160×0.9=144). A subtracts 30% flat; B subtracts 40% flat; C applies only the first discount.

Q32 — Rate Comparison (Medium)

Printer A: 60 pages/4 min. Printer B: 100 pages/5 min. Faster?

  • A. B (20 ppm > 15 ppm)
  • B. A (15 ppm > 20 ppm)
  • C. Equal
  • D. Neither prints
Answer: A. 15 vs 20 ppm). B reverses; C equates; D is absurd.

Q33 — Fraction of Remainder (Medium)

A tank is 3/4 full. After using 1/3 of the water, what fraction remains?

  • A. 1/2
  • B. 5/12
  • C. 2/3
  • D. 1/4
Answer: A. 3/4 × 2/3 = 6/12 = 1/2). B subtracts 1/3 flat; C keeps 2/3 of full; D halves the start.

Q34 — Estimate Total (Easy)

About 30 students per class, 22 classes. Best estimate of total?

  • A. 520
  • B. 900
  • C. 300
  • D. 660
Answer: D. 30×22 = 660). A uses ~24/class; B uses ~40; C counts classes only.

Q35 — Correlation vs Causation (Hard)

Ice-cream sales and drownings rise together in summer. Which conclusion is valid?

  • A. Ice cream causes drowning.
  • B. Drowning causes ice-cream sales.
  • C. The data show no relationship.
  • D. A third factor (season/heat) likely drives both; no causation shown.
Answer: D. Classic confound; correlation ≠ causation. A/B assert causation; C denies the visible link.
