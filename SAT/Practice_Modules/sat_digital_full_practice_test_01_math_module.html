❓ Math Problems (Questions 1–22)

Question 1 (Algebra: Linear Function Modeling)

A commercial solar installation company charges a flat equipment consultation fee of $\$450$ plus $\$125$ per solar panel installed. If a commercial client was billed a total of $\$5{,}700$, how many solar panels were installed?

  • A) $38$
  • B) $42$
  • C) $45$
  • D) $49$

Question 2 (Algebra: Linear Inequality)

Which of the following points $(x, y)$ satisfies the system of inequalities below?

$$\begin{cases}

y > 2x - 5 \\

y \le -x + 4

\end{cases}$$

  • A) $(3, 2)$
  • B) $(0, -6)$
  • C) $(1, 1)$
  • D) $(2, 5)$

Question 3 (Algebra: System of Linear Equations - No Solution)

In the given system of equations, $a$ and $b$ are constants.

$$\begin{cases}

4x - 6y = 15 \\

ax + 9y = b

\end{cases}$$

If the system has no solution, which of the following could be the values of $a$ and $b$?

  • A) $a = -6$ and $b = -22.5$
  • B) $a = -6$ and $b = 10$
  • C) $a = 6$ and $b = -22.5$
  • D) $a = 6$ and $b = 15$

Question 4 (Algebra: Rearranging Literal Equations)

The formula for the electric power $P$ dissipated through a resistor is given by:

$$P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

where $V$ is voltage and $R$ is electrical resistance. Which of the following correctly expresses $V$ in terms of $P$ and $R$, assuming all quantities are positive?

  • A) $V = \sqrt{P \cdot R}$
  • B) $V = \frac{P}{R^2}$
  • C) $V = (P \cdot R)^2$
  • D) $V = \sqrt{\frac{P}{R}}$

Question 5 (Algebra: Parallel & Perpendicular Lines)

Line $L_1$ in the $xy$-plane passes through the points $(-2, 5)$ and $(4, -1)$. Line $L_2$ is perpendicular to line $L_1$ and passes through the point $(3, 7)$. What is the $y$-intercept of line $L_2$?

  • A) $(0, 4)$
  • B) $(0, 3)$
  • C) $(0, -4)$
  • D) $(0, 10)$

Question 6 (Algebra: Absolute Value Equation)

What is the sum of all real solutions to the equation $|2x - 7| = 13$?

  • A) $3$
  • B) $7$
  • C) $10$
  • D) $14$

Question 7 (Advanced Math: Quadratic Vertex Minimum)

The function $f(x) = 3x^2 - 24x + 55$ models the height, in meters, of a drone relative to a canyon floor $x$ seconds after launch. What is the minimum height reached by the drone?

  • A) $4\text{ meters}$
  • B) $7\text{ meters}$
  • C) $12\text{ meters}$
  • D) $55\text{ meters}$

Question 8 (Advanced Math: Equivalent Polynomial Expressions)

Which of the following is equivalent to the expression $\frac{2x^3 - 5x^2 + 8x - 12}{x - 2}$ for all $x \ne 2$?

  • A) $2x^2 - x + 6$
  • B) $2x^2 - x + 6 + \frac{0}{x - 2}$
  • C) $2x^2 - 9x + 26 - \frac{64}{x - 2}$
  • D) $2x^2 - x + 6$ (Exact division with remainder $0$)

Question 9 (Advanced Math: Quadratic Formula & Radical Form)

If $x > 0$ and $x^2 - 6x - 11 = 0$, what is the value of $x$?

  • A) $3 + 2\sqrt{5}$
  • B) $3 + 4\sqrt{5}$
  • C) $6 + \sqrt{20}$
  • D) $3 + \sqrt{11}$

Question 10 (Advanced Math: Exponent Rules with Radicals)

For all positive values of $y$, the expression $\sqrt[3]{y^5} \cdot \sqrt{y^3}$ can be written in the form $y^k$, where $k$ is a constant. What is the value of $k$?

  • A) $\frac{19}{6}$
  • B) $\frac{15}{6}$
  • C) $\frac{8}{5}$
  • D) $\frac{5}{2}$

Question 11 (Advanced Math: Nonlinear Systems of Equations)

How many distinct real intersection points do the graphs of $y = x^2 - 4x + 5$ and $y = 2x - 4$ share in the $xy$-plane?

  • A) Zero
  • B) Exactly one
  • C) Exactly two
  • D) Infinitely many

Question 12 (Advanced Math: Exponential Decay Function)

A radioactive isotope sample has an initial mass of $640\text{ grams}$. The mass of the isotope decreases by $50\%$ every $14$ days. Which equation models the remaining mass $M(t)$, in grams, after $t$ days?

  • A) $M(t) = 640(0.5)^{14t}$
  • B) $M(t) = 640(0.5)^{\frac{t}{14}}$
  • C) $M(t) = 640(2)^{\frac{t}{14}}$
  • D) $M(t) = 14(0.5)^{\frac{t}{640}}$

Question 13 (Advanced Math: Function Transformations)

The graph of $y = g(x)$ is obtained by shifting the graph of $f(x) = (x + 3)^2 - 5$ three units to the right and four units upwards. What is the equation of $g(x)$?

  • A) $g(x) = x^2 - 1$
  • B) $g(x) = (x + 6)^2 - 1$
  • C) $g(x) = x^2 - 9$
  • D) $g(x) = (x - 3)^2 + 1$

Question 14 (Data Analysis: Two-Way Probability Table)

A software firm surveyed 150 software engineers regarding their primary programming language and their work arrangement.

Work Arrangement Python TypeScript / JavaScript Rust Total
Fully Remote 45 35 10 90
Hybrid / On-site 20 30 10 60
Total 65 65 20 150

If an engineer who codes primarily in Python is chosen at random, what is the probability that this engineer works Fully Remote?

  • A) $\frac{45}{150}$
  • B) $\frac{45}{90}$
  • C) $\frac{45}{65}$
  • D) $\frac{65}{150}$

Question 15 (Data Analysis: Percentage Increase & Unit Conversion)

A manufacturing plant produced $12{,}000$ microchips in May. In June, production increased by $25\%$. In July, production decreased by $10\%$ relative to June. How many microchips were produced in July?

  • A) $13{,}500$
  • B) $13{,}800$
  • C) $14{,}200$
  • D) $15{,}000$

Question 16 (Data Analysis: Scatterplot Line of Best Fit)

The scatterplot below shows the relationship between ambient outdoor temperature $x$ (in $^\circ\text{C}$) and hourly electricity consumption $y$ (in kilowatt-hours) for an office building. The line of best fit is given by:

$$\hat{y} = 1.85x + 42.0$$

Based on this model, what is the predicted electricity consumption, in kilowatt-hours, when the ambient temperature is $32^\circ\text{C}$?

  • A) $98.2\text{ kWh}$
  • B) $101.2\text{ kWh}$
  • C) $104.5\text{ kWh}$
  • D) $112.0\text{ kWh}$

Question 17 (Data Analysis: Statistical Standard Deviation & Spread)

Dataset $A$ consists of the numbers $\{10, 10, 10, 10, 10\}$. Dataset $B$ consists of the numbers $\{6, 8, 10, 12, 14\}$. Which of the following statements comparing their standard deviations ($\sigma_A$ and $\sigma_B$) is true?

  • A) $\sigma_A > \sigma_B$
  • B) $\sigma_A = \sigma_B$
  • C) $\sigma_A = 0$ and $\sigma_B > 0$
  • D) The standard deviations cannot be determined without sample weights.

💡 Dual Solutions, Distractor Rationales & Explanations

Question 1

  • Algebraic: $450 + 125n = 5700 \implies 125n = 5250 \implies n = 42$.
  • Desmos: Type 450 + 125n = 5700 into line 1. Desmos plots vertical line at $x = 42$.

Question 2

  • Algebraic: Test $(1, 1)$:

- $1 > 2(1) - 5 \implies 1 > -3$ (True)

- $1 \le -(1) + 4 \implies 1 \le 3$ (True)

  • Desmos: Graph y > 2x - 5 and y <= -x + 4. The point $(1, 1)$ falls inside the double-shaded overlapping feasible region.

Question 3

  • Algebraic: For no solution, slopes must match ($\frac{4}{a} = \frac{-6}{9} \implies -6a = 36 \implies a = -6$) but $y$-intercepts must differ ($\frac{15}{b} \ne -\frac{2}{3} \implies b \ne -22.5$). Choice B has $a = -6$ and $b = 10$.
  • Desmos: Graph 4x - 6y = 15 and ax + 9y = b with sliders. When $a = -6$ and $b = 10$, the lines are parallel and never intersect.

Question 4

  • Algebraic: $P = \frac{V^2}{R} \implies V^2 = P \cdot R \implies V = \sqrt{P \cdot R}$.

Question 5

  • Algebraic:

- Slope $m_1 = \frac{-1 - 5}{4 - (-2)} = \frac{-6}{6} = -1$.

- Perpendicular slope $m_2 = -\frac{1}{-1} = 1$.

- Equation of $L_2$: $y - 7 = 1(x - 3) \implies y = x + 4$. The $y$-intercept is $(0, 4)$.

Question 6

  • Algebraic: $2x - 7 = 13 \implies 2x = 20 \implies x = 10$, or $2x - 7 = -13 \implies 2x = -6 \implies x = -3$.

$$\text{Sum} = 10 + (-3) = 7$$

Question 7

  • Algebraic:

- Vertex $x = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{-24}{2(3)} = 4$.

- Minimum height $f(4) = 3(4)^2 - 24(4) + 55 = 48 - 96 + 55 = 7\text{ meters}$.

  • Desmos: Type f(x) = 3x^2 - 24x + 55. Click the minimum vertex point at $(4, 7)$. The $y$-value is $7$.

Question 8

  • Algebraic: Synthetic division of $(2x^3 - 5x^2 + 8x - 12)$ by $(x - 2)$:

$$\text{Quotient: } 2x^2 - x + 6\text{ with Remainder } 0$$

  • Desmos: Graph $y_1 = \frac{2x^3 - 5x^2 + 8x - 12}{x - 2}$ and $y_2 = 2x^2 - x + 6$. The two parabolas overlap perfectly across all points.

Question 9

  • Algebraic:

$$x = \frac{-(-6) \pm \sqrt{(-6)^2 - 4(1)(-11)}}{2(1)} = \frac{6 \pm \sqrt{36 + 44}}{2} = \frac{6 \pm \sqrt{80}}{2} = \frac{6 \pm 4\sqrt{5}}{2} = 3 + 2\sqrt{5}\text{ (since } x > 0)$$

Question 10

  • Algebraic:

$$y^{\frac{5}{3}} \cdot y^{\frac{3}{2}} = y^{\frac{5}{3} + \frac{3}{2}} = y^{\frac{10 + 9}{6}} = y^{\frac{19}{6}} \implies k = \frac{19}{6}$$

Question 11

  • Algebraic: Equate expressions:

$$x^2 - 4x + 5 = 2x - 4 \implies x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0 \implies (x - 3)^2 = 0 \implies x = 3$$

Discriminant $\Delta = (-6)^2 - 4(1)(9) = 0$, indicating exactly one tangent intersection point at $(3, 2)$.

  • Desmos: Graph both equations; they touch at single tangent point $(3, 2)$.

Question 12

  • Algebraic: Standard half-life equation: $M(t) = M_0 \cdot (0.5)^{\frac{t}{h}}$, where $M_0 = 640$ and half-life $h = 14$ days $\implies M(t) = 640(0.5)^{\frac{t}{14}}$.

Question 13

  • Algebraic:

- Original: $f(x) = (x + 3)^2 - 5$ with vertex at $(-3, -5)$.

- Shift right 3: $x$-coordinate becomes $-3 + 3 = 0$.

- Shift up 4: $y$-coordinate becomes $-5 + 4 = -1$.

- New vertex form: $g(x) = (x - 0)^2 - 1 = x^2 - 1$.

Question 14

  • Algebraic: Denominator is all Python users ($65$). Numerator is remote Python users ($45$). Probability is $\frac{45}{65} = \frac{9}{13}$.

Question 15

  • Algebraic:

- June: $12000 \times 1.25 = 15000$.

- July: $15000 \times 0.90 = 13500$.

Question 16

  • Algebraic: $\hat{y} = 1.85(32) + 42.0 = 59.2 + 42.0 = 101.2\text{ kWh}$.

Question 17

  • Algebraic: Dataset $A$ has identical values, so variance and standard deviation are strictly $0$ ($\sigma_A = 0$). Dataset $B$ has spread, so $\sigma_B > 0$.

Question 18

  • Algebraic: Ratio equality for infinite solutions:

$$\frac{6}{-9} = \frac{-8}{m} \implies 6m = (-9)(-8) = 72 \implies m = \frac{72}{6} = 12$$

Question 19

  • Algebraic: Complete the square:

$$(x^2 - 12x + 36) + (y^2 + 16y + 64) = -19 + 36 + 64 = 81$$

$$(x - 6)^2 + (y + 8)^2 = 81 \implies r = \sqrt{81} = 9$$

Question 20

  • Algebraic: In a 5-12-13 right triangle where $\cos(P) = \frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}} = \frac{12}{13}$, opposite side $= \sqrt{13^2 - 12^2} = 5$.

$$\tan(P) = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}} = \frac{5}{12}$$

Question 21

  • Algebraic: By Vieta's formulas, for $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, the sum of roots is:

$$x_1 + x_2 = -\frac{b}{a} = -\frac{-21}{3} = \frac{21}{3} = 7$$

Question 22

  • Algebraic: Cylinder volume $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi (4)^2 (9) = \pi (16)(9) = 144\pi \implies k = 144$.
